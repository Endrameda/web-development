# Inbox

> Capture zone. Anything I hit during work, reading, or watching lands here in under 10 seconds. Sorting happens weekly, not now.

## Capture rule

Do not format it. Do not tag it. Do not decide where it belongs. Deciding is the friction that
makes people stop capturing, and a system you stop feeding is worth nothing.

Minimum viable capture — a new file, or one line appended to an existing one, whichever is faster:

```
<link, or the thing in one line>
why: <half a sentence>
```

The `why` line is the only part that matters. Future-me cannot reconstruct it, and a bare link
with no reason attached always gets deleted at review time anyway.

## Weekly review — 20 minutes, timeboxed

Every item gets exactly one of four outcomes. There is no fifth option and no "leave it for next
week":

1. **Delete.** It was interesting for ten seconds and isn't anymore. This should be the most
   common outcome by a wide margin. An inbox that only grows is a hoard.
2. **Log it** in [Reading](../reading/0.%20Reading.md) — read it, took one line from it, done.
   Fill the *Fed into* column or admit it changed nothing.
3. **Promote** to a real note in the right folder: add the `>` summary line, tags, status, and a
   `## Sources` entry.
4. **Append** to an existing note as a gotcha, an example, or a source.

Then check whether the folder's `0. …` index still describes reality — new note in the table, item
struck off Gaps.

Stop at 20 minutes even if the inbox isn't empty. Anything that survives two reviews untouched
gets deleted on the third: two weeks of not writing it is the honest answer about whether you
were ever going to.
