# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

> A technique that improves an LLM's answers by retrieving relevant data from an external or internal knowledge base and injecting it into the prompt before generation — company docs, internal wikis, anything the model was never trained on or that changes too often to retrain for.
